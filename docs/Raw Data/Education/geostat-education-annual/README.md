# Annual education statistics (National Statistics Office of Georgia)

Research package for the Fiscal.ge "Education" hub. Approved design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-education-design.md`. Everything here is reproducible from the files in `official/`, which are never edited. The school tables (01, 06 and 11) and the vocational table end with "Source: Ministry of Education, Science and Youth of Georgia."; the kindergarten and higher education workbooks name no source.

## Outputs

- `education-annual.csv`: the 8,815 served observations, one row per indicator, place and year, with columns `year,period_label,indicator_id,geography_id,value,published_value,unit,basis,value_status,shown,source_id,source_sheet,source_cell`.
  - `year` is a school year's starting year (2025 for 2025/26), or the calendar year for vocational rows; `period_label` is `2025/26` or `2025`.
  - `value` is the exact decimal token stored in the cell; a text cell is read with its spaces removed (table 01 Z3, `2 294`, is the only one served). `published_value` is the value as the cell displays it: the stored double to 15 significant digits, then rounded half-up to the decimals of the cell's number format, which must be one for `persons_thousand` and none for `persons` and `institutions` (a `General` cell may not hold more). For example the 2012/13 pupils cell stores `559.41499999999996`, shows `559.415` and is published as `559.4`.
  - `unit` is `institutions` (schools, kindergartens), `persons_thousand` (Georgia's pupils) or `persons`. `basis` is always `actual`, `value_status` always `official`: nothing is calculated, imputed or adjusted.
  - `shown` is `false` only for the 9 old vocational fields (170 rows); they are kept for the sums and never reach a page.
- `education-series-breaks.csv`: `kindergarten_staff_2020` (`education.kg_staff.total`, from `2020-09-01`) and `he_levels_2015` (the six `education.he_*.level_bachelor` and `.level_master` IDs, from `2015-09-01`), in demography's break format (see Notes).
- `coverage.csv`: first and last year, count and any unavailable years inside that range for each of the 712 indicator–place series.
- `validation-report.json` (from `prepare.py`) and `independent-validation.json` (from `verify_independent.mjs`).
- `source-manifest.csv` and `source-manifest.json`.
- Place labels are mapped in the reviewed `data/mappings/education/units.csv`; Geostat's own sum mismatches are recorded in the reviewed `data/mappings/education/known-differences.csv`.

## Cell map

`prepare.py` finds every column by its printed year heading (and the heading under it) and every row by its printed label in column A, never by fixed position. Labels are compared with runs of spaces collapsed and ends trimmed (`City of Kutaisi `, `Education `).

- **Table 01, sheet `1`.** Years from the row of `2000/2001` … `2025/2026` headings. Rows `General education schools, unit`, `Public general education schools`, `Private general education schools`, `Number of pupils - thsd.`, `In public general education schools`, `In private general education schools` → `schools` and `pupils_national` `total`, `public`, `private`.
- **Table 06, sheet `1`.** Years `2006/2007` … `2025/2026`. Rows `Teachers, total`, `In Public schools`, `In Private schools` → `teachers` `total`, `public`, `private`. The two `of which females` rows and sheet `2` are not served.
- **Table 11.** Sheets `schools` and `pupils`: one column per school year (`2005/2006` … `2025/2026`, 2010/11 printed `2010/2011*`). Sheet `teachers`: a Total/Women/Men triple under each year heading `2016/2017` … `2025/2026`. Rows are every label in column A below the headings, mapped through `units.csv` (`GEORGIA – total`, the 11 regions in capitals, `ABKHAZIA AR`, `City of …` and `… Municipality` rows, `Eredvi`, `Tighva`, `QurTa`); `* As at 5 April 2011`, `Note:` and `Source:` rows are footnotes. A label `units.csv` does not list stops preparation, as does a place printed twice or under another region than its own. The `teachers` sheet prints no Eredvi, Tighva or Kurta row. Georgia's row serves only teachers by sex; its schools, pupils and teachers totals are cross-checks against tables 01 and 06.
- **Kindergarten table, sheet `1`.** A triple under each year heading `2017/2018` … `2025/2026`, by heading: `The number of PEC Institutions` → `kindergartens`, `The number of children involved in PEC services` → `kg_children`, `The number of caregiver-pedagogues/caregivers` (to 2019/20) or `The number of caregiver-pedagogues/caregivers/assistant caregivers/Inclusive education special teachers` (from 2020/21; any other year stops) → `kg_staff`. Rows as table 11 (no Eredvi, Tighva or Kurta); the footnote is `1)Actually operating institutions during the accounting period.`
- **Higher education workbook**, sheets `Number` → `he_students`, `Admission` → `he_admitted`, `Graduates` → `he_graduates`. Each sheet holds three blocks, found by their titles: "… in Public and Private Institutions by Programmes and Sex" (`Public And Private` on `Admission`), "… in Public Institutions …" and "… in Private Institutions …". Under each title: the year headings (`2011-2012 school year` …, or `2011 year` … `2025 year` on `Graduates`), then `Total` / `of which`, then the three level headings of each year (`Bachelor or Certified physician programme` / `Bachelor programme` → `level_bachelor`, `Magistracy or Residency` / `Magistracy, Certified physician (Veterinarian) or Residency` → `level_master`, `Professional Programme` → `level_professional`; the old headings must stop and the new ones start in 2015/16, or in the "2015 year" column for graduates). Rows: the total row (`Number of students, total`, `Number of students admitted, total`, `Graduates , total`), the 8 field rows under it (`Education`, `Humanities and Arts`, `Social sciences, business and law`, `Science`, `Engineering, manufacturing and construction`, `Agriculture`, `Health and welfare`, `Services`), `of which females` and `of which males` (their own field rows are not served). Served: the Public-and-Private block's total row (Total and the three level columns), its field rows and its female and male totals (Total column), and the Public and Private blocks' total-row Total cells → `public`, `private`.
- **Vocational workbook, sheet `1`.** Year headings are numbers (`2013` … `2025`); under each, `Number of admitted students` → `vet_admitted` and `Number of graduated students` → `vet_graduates`, each with Total/Women/Men. Rows: `Number of Students, total` (Total, Women, Men) and 18 field rows (Total column): the current list `Agriculture, forestry, fisheries, veterinary` → `field_agriculture_vet`, `Art, humanitarian sciences` → `field_arts_humanities`, `Business, Administration and Law` → `field_business_law`, `Education` → `field_education`, `Engineering, Manufacturing, Construction` → `field_engineering`, `Health, Social welfare` → `field_health_welfare`, `ICT` → `field_ict`, `Services` → `field_services`, `Social Sciences, Journalism and information` → `field_social_journalism`; the old list `Agricultural Sciences`, `Business Administration`, `Engineering`, `Science / Natural Sciences`, `Inter directional branches and specialties`, `Law`, `Social Sciences`, `Art`, `Health` → `old_agricultural_sciences`, `old_business_administration`, `old_engineering`, `old_natural_sciences`, `old_inter_directional`, `old_law`, `old_social_sciences`, `old_art`, `old_health`. The age rows below `Of which:` are archived, not served.

**Unavailable cells.** `…`, `...`, `-` and blank cells produce no row, never a zero. `UNAVAILABLE` in `prepare.py` lists every one inside a served range (196 cells), and any other unavailable cell, or a value in a listed one, stops preparation: table 06 private teachers 2010/11 (F7, `...`); Akhalgori's schools and pupils from 2009/10 and its teachers in every year; Eredvi's schools and pupils in 2008/09 and from 2011/12; Tighva's and Kurta's from 2008/09; Abkhazia AR's kindergartens, children and staff in every year (`…`).

**Vocational field spans.** A field's active span runs from the first to the last year in which either measure's Total is non-zero; Geostat's zeros outside it produce no row. The spans are recorded in `VET_SPANS` and must match the workbook: current fields 2019–2025, except Education 2016–2025 and Social sciences, journalism and information 2020–2025; old fields 2013–2022, except Science / Natural Sciences 2013–2019 and Law 2013–2020. Inside a span a zero is served as `0` (31 rows, 27 of them old fields; the shown ones are the first-year graduates of Education 2016 and of Agriculture, Arts and Health 2019). The vocational and university cells use an accounting number format that displays a zero as `-`; those cells still store the number 0, unlike the text `-` marker.

## Checks

`python3 prepare.py` rebuilds every generated file and fails unless all of these hold; `python3 prepare.py --check` fails if a committed file differs from a rebuild. `python3 -m unittest test_validation -v` runs the regression tests, including negative controls.

- Every served (indicator, place, year) is present exactly once and nothing else (8,815 rows), every value equals its source cell's token, re-read, every published value is that value rounded to the cell's number format, and every period label and `shown` flag follows its measure and group.
- Parts add up to the printed total exactly (Georgia's pupils in thousands within 0.15), except the mismatches recorded in `data/mappings/education/known-differences.csv`, each of which must recur with exactly its recorded difference:
  - public + private = total for Georgia's schools, pupils and teachers (all years with both parts) and for each university measure;
  - women + men = total for teachers in Georgia and every place, and for each university and vocational measure;
  - the three levels and the eight fields each = total for each university measure;
  - old + current vocational fields = total every year, and the current fields alone from 2023 (in 2019–2022 they fall short by admitted 5,696, 119, 0, 0 and graduates 10,434, 2,079, 421, 546, recorded in `validation-report.json`);
  - the municipalities and kept units under a region = the region row, and the 11 regions + Abkhazia AR = Georgia's row, for schools, pupils, teachers (total, women, men), kindergartens, children and staff.
- Recorded mismatches (Geostat's own rows, served as printed): kindergarten children 2024/25, Samegrelo-Zemo Svaneti prints 9,836 but its municipalities add up to 9,436 (−400), and so the regions exceed Georgia's 137,074 by 400; pupils 2022/23, Guria prints 14,490 but Lanchkhuti, Ozurgeti and Chokhatauri add up to 14,695 (+205), while Georgia's row agrees with Guria's.
- Table 11's own Georgia row: its pupils ÷ 1,000 round to table 01's thousands every year (644,243 → 644.2 in 2025/26), its schools equal table 01 and its teachers equal table 06 from 2016/17 (52 year comparisons).
- Graduates are stored one year back: "2011 year" … "2025 year" → 2010 … 2024; the 2024 row is the "2025 year" column, 32,494.
- Anchors, as published: 2025/26 schools 2,292 (206 private), pupils 644.2 thousand, Tbilisi pupils 232,134, teachers 66,328, kindergartens 1,717, children 130,337, staff 19,176, university students 195,426, admitted 58,554; graduates of 2025 32,494; vocational 2025 admitted 19,690 and graduates 10,537.

`node verify_independent.mjs` re-reads the six served workbooks with SheetJS (the `xlsx` package of `apps/web`) and, with label tables written separately from `prepare.py` and the places from `units.csv`, re-extracts every served cell: its year heading, the Total/Women/Men, measure or level heading, the row label, the block title, the unavailable markers and the vocational spans. Every CSV row must match that re-extraction (cell, stored value, rounding to the number format, unit, period label, flags) and nothing may be missing. Its negative controls (altered value, omitted row, shifted year, neighbouring column, wrong place row, swapped sheets, graduate year not shifted, unavailable cell served, zero outside a field's span served, old field shown, wrong unit, unrounded published value) must all be detected.

## Notes

- **2010/11 count date.** Table 11 heads 2010/11 `2010/2011*` with the footnote "* As at 5 April 2011"; the year is served like any other.
- **Teachers counted per school.** Tables 06 and 11: "Note: Teachers employed at more than one school are counted as many times as the number of schools in which they teach."
- **Public kindergartens only.** The kindergarten table covers public preschool education and care institutions; its footnote: "1)Actually operating institutions during the accounting period."
- **Kindergarten staff from 2020/21** (series break `kindergarten_staff_2020`): the staff heading widens from "The number of caregiver-pedagogues/caregivers" to "The number of caregiver-pedagogues/caregivers/assistant caregivers/Inclusive education special teachers". No change may be computed across 2019/20–2020/21.
- **Master's level from 2015/16** (series break `he_levels_2015`): "Note: From 2015-2016 school year in accordance with the International Standard Classification of Education (ISCED 2011), certified doctor (veterinarian) program, residency - are included in the Masters level." (sheet `Number`, A103; `Admission` A103 says "From 2015/2016 school year", `Graduates` A164 "From 2015"). Because graduates are stored one year back, their new headings start at stored year 2014 (the "2015 year" column), a year before the break's reference date.
- **Graduates' year.** Geostat publishes graduates by the calendar year they graduated; each is stored under the school year it ends (2025 graduates under 2024/25), so graduates run 2010/11–2024/25.
- **Vocational fields.** Geostat changed its list of fields in 2019 and phased the old list out by 2022 (the last old-field graduates are in 2022, the last admissions in 2020). The old fields are kept with `shown=false`; in 2019–2022 some students are still counted under them, so the current fields add up to less than the total in those years.
- **Occupied territories.** Abkhazia AR, Akhalgori, Eredvi, Tighva and Kurta have rows in Geostat's tables and are part of the region and Georgia totals; they are kept with their own IDs and are not places on the map.
