## Decisions preserved

- Monetary values retain their full XML decimal tokens. For example, national 2025 exports are 7287.8050275742908 million USD and 2024 service exports are 7706284.9847599976 thousand USD. Initial openpyxl previews were slightly rounded.
- Regional SITC numeric identifiers can contain binary storage noise (B10 stores 1.1000000000000001). Their published four-digit convention yields 001.1; this code-only formatting does not round monetary values. Both the independent preview and original stored code tokens are recorded.
- Early HS6 annual sheets for 2000-2008 publish codes without descriptions. Descriptions remain empty rather than copied from another classification block.
- Regional legal-entity/private-person rows and unlabeled joint-service control cells are supporting observations. They stay in source-observation evidence and are excluded from prepared product/service detail.
- Goods national monthly rows, all separate monthly/quarterly worksheets, and 2026 periods are excluded from preparation, while originals remain unchanged.
- Domestic detail retains its selected list and Other commodities. Unknown-region product detail is not published. Service residuals and symbols remain distinct from numerical zero.
- Country table EU/CIS/Other sections are subtotals over their own published rows. Separate EU/CIS/BSEC/OECD/GUAM aggregates overlap and do not imply equivalent membership.
- Historical product blocks are retained as distinct identities. Same-year matches will be explicitly reviewed rather than inferred across all years.
