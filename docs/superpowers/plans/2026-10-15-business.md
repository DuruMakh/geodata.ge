# Business Hub Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship the bilingual Business hub (`/explorer/business` with `/sales`, `/jobs`, `/investment-profit`, `/firms`, `/regions`, `/regions/[id]` for Georgia and 11 regions, and `/methodology/business`), serving about 4,600 reviewed Geostat business-statistics values.

**Architecture:** Follows Money and banks (PR #177, merge `1eaab3f6`; `git show 1eaab3f6 --stat` lists every shared file it touched) for the data pipeline and topic pages, and Living standards Income (`components/household/household-index.tsx`, `household-explorer.tsx`, `lib/pages/household-*.tsx`, `lib/explorer/householdRegionMap.ts`) for the Regions index and area pages. The parts are an immutable research package, a TypeScript prepare/check step writing one canonical CSV, a loader with CSV/DB parity, a private Prisma mirror table, the shared `topicSeries` model, and two client components: one for the four topic pages and one for the area pages.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16, strict TypeScript, Tailwind v4, Prisma 7 / Supabase, Vitest, Playwright, Python 3 stdlib (research package), Node (independent verifier).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-business-design.md` (approved by Duru on 2026-10-15, 09:51 Tbilisi).

**Branch:** `claude/project-thread-g0x752`, from `origin/main` at `b0bc5194`; the spec is already committed there.

## Global Constraints

- Yearly values only. The last year is 2025 (firms: 2024). Geostat's 2026 quarterly rows are never served.
- Under each page heading, only the one-line `LatestValueLine`. No summary block, and nothing under the chart except one `SourceNote` line.
- No tab rows. Unit changes use the icon-only `SegmentedTabs` measure switch with `ControlTooltip` (DESIGN §7.2a).
- Only the spec's default series are selected on arrival. Those are All firms (Sales, Jobs), Investment, New firms plus Closed firms (Firms), and Sales, Jobs or New plus Closed firms on area pages. Defaults stay first, selectable and removable. Selection is unlimited, and search never scopes the bulk action or the count.
- Amounts are stored in million GEL and shown in billion GEL (`unitsFor(locale).bn`, `format.bnGel`). People are shown in thousands to one decimal, firm counts as whole numbers and rates as percent to one decimal.
- Missing means a gap, a dash or a blank Excel cell, never zero. Geostat's `…`/`...` cells produce no row.
- Fiscal.ge computes nothing: no net profit, shares or growth. Every row has `value_status = official`.
- Regions use the site's 11 region IDs from `REGIONAL_ECONOMY_REGIONS`. Region rows exist only from 2006 for money and jobs. Abkhazeti AR and "unknown" exist only in the firms data and are never drawn.
- Stable lowercase ASCII IDs `business.{measure}.{group}` (spec §9). Labels are display data.
- The canonical CSV is UTF-8 with BOM. The methodology bundle must not pull large data files (PR #170 rule).
- No MCP tools or central JSON/CSV publications. The fact-query snapshot must not include business sources.
- Review before pushing: commit locally only. Screenshots, and on request a pushed-branch preview, go to Duru (Task 9). Full gates and the PR come only after Duru's OK (Task 11).
- Times told to Duru are in Tbilisi time (UTC+4).

## Review Focus

1. **Geostat text-number cells** (`'5054'`, `'1 499'`, and doubles like `25.86389982551932`): the value is parsed exactly and published at the cell's displayed precision (15 significant digits, then half-up), never misread or shifted. Owner: Task 1 (`test_text_numbers`, `test_display_rounding`).
2. **Industry gaps in early years** (`…` cells): the chart shows a gap, the table a dash and Excel a blank cell. Selecting the total alongside keeps its full 1999 start. Owner: Task 4 (model) and Task 7 (browser).
3. **The area page measure in a saved link** (`#m=firms&sel=business.turnover.region_guria`): series from another measure are dropped. An unknown `m` falls back to money, and a range starting before 2014 clamps to 2014 on firms. Owner: Task 4.
4. **Rate measure with Active firms selected:** switching to Rate drops Active firms and keeps New or Closed rows, including nested sections. Switching back to Number keeps them, and an empty result falls back to the default. Owner: Task 4.
5. **Language switch and region picker** keep measure, range, selection and chart/table mode on topic and area pages. Owner: Task 7 (browser).
