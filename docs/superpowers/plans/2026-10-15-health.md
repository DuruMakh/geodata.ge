> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship the bilingual Health and social protection hub with its three Health pages and the methodology page, serving about 4,200 reviewed Geostat health and causes-of-death values.

- Hub: `/explorer/health`.
- Pages: `/doctors-hospitals`, `/illnesses` and `/causes-of-death`, plus `/causes-of-death/regions/[id]` for Georgia and the 11 regions.
- Methodology: `/methodology/health`.

**Architecture:**
- **Data pipeline and the two national pages** follow Energy and food (PR #175, merge `b63eb98f`). `git show b63eb98f --stat` lists every shared file it touched.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16, strict TypeScript, Tailwind v4, Prisma 7 / Supabase, Vitest, Playwright, Python 3 stdlib (research package), Node (independent verifier).

## Global Constraints

- **Yearly values only.** Illnesses and staff run 2002–2025; diabetes and anaemia 2016–2025; deaths and death rates 2014–2025.
- **Page text.** Under each page heading there is only the one-line `LatestValueLine`. Nothing sits under the chart except one `SourceNote` line.
- **No tab rows.** The Doctors and hospitals views use the icon-only `SegmentedTabs` measure switch with `ControlTooltip` (DESIGN §7.2a). Icons are Lucide `Users`, `Hospital`, `BedDouble` and `Stethoscope`.
- **Defaults:**
  - Doctors and hospitals: Doctors; on switching view, Hospitals, Hospital beds or Out-patient visits.
  - Illnesses: the five largest disease groups in the latest year, derived from the loaded facts.
  - Area pages: All deaths.
  - Defaults stay first, selectable and removable. Selection is unlimited, and search never scopes the bulk action or the count.
- **Missing values.** Missing means a gap, a dash or a blank Excel cell, never zero. Geostat's `…`/`...` cells produce no row. The death-rate `-` cells belong only to Abkhazia, which is not served. `0` in the deaths table is a published count and is served.
- **Computed values.** Fiscal.ge computes nothing: no totals of new cases, shares, rates per cause or growth. Unit conversion for display (thousand ↔ count, thousand ↔ million) is exact multiplication by a power of ten. Excel exports the stored unit.
- **Counting changes.** `staff_definitions_2012`, `staff_register_2020` and `new_cases_electronic_2020` are drawn with `EditorialLineChart` `breaks` (gap plus dashed rule, label "new counting") and `ExplorerTable` `breakYears`, whenever a selected series is in the break's `appliesTo`. `icd10_groups_2006` is recorded but not drawn.
- **Area IDs.** These are `georgia` plus `WAGES_REGIONS` (`lib/data/wages/types.ts`), as Living standards uses. Abkhazia is never served.
- **Stable IDs.** Lowercase ASCII `health.{topic}.{item}` (spec §7). Labels are display data.
- **File rules.** The canonical CSV is UTF-8 with BOM, and the methodology bundle must not pull large data files (PR #170 rule).
- **Not exposed elsewhere.** No MCP tools and no central JSON/CSV publications. The fact-query snapshot must not include health sources.
- **Review before pushing.** Commit locally only. Screenshots, and on request a pushed-branch preview, go to Duru in Task 9. Full gates and the PR come only after Duru's OK, in Task 11.
- **Times.** Times told to Duru are in Tbilisi time (UTC+4).

## Review Focus

1. **The 2019→2020 staff drop.** Doctors fall from 2019 to 2020 only because of the new register. With Doctors selected, the chart shows a gap and a "new counting" rule at 2020, the table marks 2020, and a range of 2021–2025 (no break inside) draws no rule. Owner: Task 4 (`buildHealthModel(...).breaks`) and Task 7 (browser).
2. **Nurse sub-groups that stop in 2011.** Ticking "Doctor's assistants" shows 2002–2011 and gaps after. Total nurses keeps its full span, and Excel has blank cells. Owner: Task 4 and Task 5.
3. **The combined nervous-system group.** "Nervous system and sense organs" exists only for 2002–2005, and nervous, eye and ear start in 2006. Ticking all four gives no overlapping years and no zeros. Owner: Task 1 (`test_icd10_split`) and Task 4.
4. **A saved link from another view.** On Doctors and hospitals, `#tab=beds&sel=health.staff.doctors` drops the doctors ID and falls back to Hospital beds. An unknown `tab` falls back to staff. On an area page, an ID from another area is dropped. Owner: Task 4.
5. **COVID-19 before 2020.** The COVID-19 row on an area page shows nothing for 2014–2019, not zeros. Causes still add up to All deaths in 2020–2022, which validates that COVID-19 is included. Owner: Task 1 (`test_causes_add_up`) and Task 4.
