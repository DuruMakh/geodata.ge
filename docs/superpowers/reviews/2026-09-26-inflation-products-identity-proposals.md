# Product identity review: August 2026 basket

**Status: proposals only. No changed-name history has been joined and no canonical product facts have been written.**

The [full candidate audit](../../../data/reports/inflation-products-identity-review.csv) preserves the English and Georgian source names, group, workbook cells and all possible predecessor clues. The [candidate catalogue](../../../data/mappings/inflation-products/catalogue.csv) assigns fixed provisional IDs to all 305 products in the current basket. Of those, 258 have an uninterrupted exact bilingual name and group match back to 2015. The other 47 reach the first boundary listed below. The proposals would link 19 wording changes and split 28 new, replaced or uncertain items. “Split” means the current product keeps its history from the later year onward; it does not erase the earlier source row.

The tables show each official English name above its Georgian counterpart. Names come from the archived Geostat workbooks, with only outer whitespace removed. A dash means no specific predecessor is proposed; the full audit still lists weaker clues where available.

A shared sheet row number or COICOP group is **never** enough to link a product. “Link” below is a human proposal based on the two source names and apparent unchanged product meaning. A changed label in even one language remains unresolved until this review is accepted. After any link, the pipeline must continue backward and reveal any earlier boundary before writing facts.

Geostat says the [2018 basket was identical to 2017’s](https://www.geostat.ge/en/single-news/1047/consumer-basket-2018). That supports basket membership continuity, but the three broadened 2018 product descriptions below still need a choice about how much earlier history to show. Geostat says the [2020 update removed two items and added one](https://geostat.ge/media/29198/30.01.2020Consumer-Basket-Update-%281%29.pdf); this aggregate count does not identify the individual histories.
