# Product identity review: approved August 2026 mapping

**Status: all first-boundary identity decisions approved and recorded. Canonical product facts have not yet been written.**

The [full candidate audit](../../../data/reports/inflation-products-identity-review.csv) preserves the English and Georgian source names, group, workbook cells and all possible predecessor clues. The [reviewed catalogue](../../../data/mappings/inflation-products/catalogue.csv) assigns fixed IDs to all 305 products in the current basket. Of those, 258 have an uninterrupted exact bilingual name and group match back to 2015. The other 47 reach the first boundary listed below. The reviewed decisions link 29 changed-name series and split 18 new or distinct items. Following those links backward leaves 287 current products with verified history to 2015 and no further unresolved boundary. “Split” means the current product keeps its history from the later year onward; it does not erase the earlier source row.

The tables show each official English name above its Georgian counterpart. Names come from the archived Geostat workbooks, with only outer whitespace removed. A dash means no specific predecessor was linked or considered; the full audit still lists weaker clues where available.

A shared sheet row number or COICOP group is **never** enough to link a product. “Link” below is a reviewed decision based on both source labels and the published index history. The pipeline follows each link backward and rejects any further boundary without an explicit decision.

Geostat says the [2018 basket was identical to 2017’s](https://www.geostat.ge/en/single-news/1047/consumer-basket-2018). The user reviewed the broadened 2018 descriptions and chose to link their published histories. Geostat says the [2020 update removed two items and added one](https://geostat.ge/media/29198/30.01.2020Consumer-Basket-Update-%281%29.pdf); this aggregate count does not identify the individual histories.
