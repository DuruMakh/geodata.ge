# Annual science and culture data collection and validation

## Purpose and status

This methodology governs the research package at `docs/Raw Data/ScienceCulture/geostat-science-culture-annual/`, collected on 2026-10-20 from Geostat's science (category 194) and culture (category 615) pages. The research captures are immutable. The 1,733 values of `data/imports/science-culture-annual.csv` are served on the Science and culture pages (Research, Patents, the Museums map index with a page for Georgia and each of the 11 regions, and Theatres), except the R&D staff women and men rows, which are stored but shown on no page and read only for the women + men = total check under the approved design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-science-culture-design.md`. Nothing is published through MCP or included in central machine-readable publications, and the science and culture sources are excluded from the fact-query snapshot.

Annual data only. Research runs 2019–2024; patents 2016–2025; museums 2005–2025 (staff 2020–2025); theatres 2006–2025 (events 2021–2025, staff 2020–2025). The sex split of each researcher degree, field and age group, patents by region (2022–2025), theatres by region and Geostat's "remaining regions" row, participants in events abroad, Geostat's averages (visitors per museum, attendance per event), scientific publications (2021 only), libraries (2001–2008), publishing and newspapers (2005–2020) and museum exhibits (2011 only) are excluded.

## Sources and coverage

Eleven Geostat workbooks supply values: `Researchers.xlsx` (sheets 1–3), `R&D-Personal.xlsx` and `Institutions.xlsx` (2019–2024); `Invention-Utility-Model.xlsx` (sheets 1 and 2, 2016–2025); `Main-charasteristics-of-Museums.xls`, `Number-of-Museums-by-Regions.xls`, `Main-charasteristics-of-Museums-by-Regions.xls` (2005–2025) and `Employed-Staff-in-Museums.xls` (2020–2025); `Main-Indicators-Of-Professional-Theatres.xls` and `Events.xls` (2006–2025) and `Employed-Staff-in-Theatres.xls` (2020–2025). The research workbooks print "Source: 2019 - GeoStat; From 2020 - Ministry of Education, Science and Youth of Georgia."; the patent workbook prints "Source: The National Intellectual Property Center of Georgia - "Sakpatenti"."; the museum and theatre workbooks name no other source.

Thirty-three originals are preserved under `official/`: the eleven served workbooks; seven other English workbooks (the two theatre-by-region workbooks, publications, museum exhibits, the two library workbooks and publishing); the 13 Georgian versions under `official/ka/`, kept for the Georgian labels; and captures of the two category pages. All 33 are registered in `data/sources/source-documents.csv` and published through `data/methodology/source-archives/science-culture.csv`. Each archive row is dated by the years it supplies (served workbooks) or prints (archived-only ones), within the pages' 2005–2025 period: as in the Health archive, the two library workbooks are dated 2005–2008 and 2005, 2007–2008, and their notes say that the 2001–2004 and 2003 columns retained inside the originals are outside that period. A Georgian version carries its English workbook's years, and a page capture the served years of its page. The by-region theatre workbook is read only for the audience cross-check; no value is read from the other archived-only workbooks or from any Georgian version.

## Research package and canonical file

The package holds the originals under `official/`, the source manifest (`source-manifest.csv` and `.json`: URL, retrieval time, SHA-256 and size of each file), `prepare.py`, which transcribes every served cell with its sheet and cell reference, the independent check `verify_independent.mjs`, the regression tests `test_validation.py`, `validation-report.json`, `independent-validation.json`, `coverage.csv` (first and last year, count and unavailable years of each of the 123 indicators), `science-culture-annual.csv` and `science-culture-series-breaks.csv`. Its README holds the cell map. Columns are found by their printed years and rows by their printed labels, never by fixed position.

Each row of the canonical file keeps the value exactly as stored in the cell (or `0` for a `-` cell in a table where `-` means none), `published_value` (counts and persons whole; thousands and million GEL at the larger of the decimals the cell shows and stores), the year, the unit (`persons`, `persons_thousand`, `count`, `count_thousand`, `gel_million`), `basis=actual`, `value_status=official`, the source ID, the sheet and the cell. The file is UTF-8 with a byte order mark.

## Research

Researchers are served in total, women and men, by four degrees (ISCED 8, 7, 6 and 5/4), seven fields of science and six age groups; all R&D staff in total, technicians, other supporting staff and not specified (its women and men rows are stored, not shown, and checked against the total); institutions that conducted R&D and their total R&D spending in million GEL. Geostat collected the 2019 figures; from 2020 they come from the Ministry of Education, Science and Youth. The pages show both as published and compute no change across them. As published, researchers under 25 rise from 170 in 2020 to 1,012 in 2021, and "Not identified" researchers are 88, 37 and 1 in 2019–2021 and `-` (0) from 2022; Geostat's tables carry no note on either.

## Patents

Sheet 1 (inventions) and sheet 2 (utility models) of `Invention-Utility-Model.xlsx` give filed applications and registered patents (shown on the pages as granted) under the national procedure, local and foreign applicants apart, and under the international procedure as one figure: groups `local`, `foreign` and `international`, shown as Georgian, Foreign and International route. No statistical tables were found on the English pages of Sakpatenti's own site, so Geostat's copy is the source. Sheet 3, patents by region (2022–2025), is archived and not served.

## Museums

Georgia's visitors and group tours (Geostat's "excursions"), both in thousands, and exhibitions come from the by-region workbook's `Georgia, total` row; Georgia's museums and the six types from the national workbook; the museums of each region from the museums-by-region workbook. The national workbook's visitors in million persons are a cross-check only. There is no Abkhazia AR row. The 11 regions add up to Georgia exactly for museums and exhibitions and within 0.2 thousand for group tours (Geostat rounds the regional tours to one decimal in early years). For visitors they add up exactly except in three years, recorded as known differences and checked to the amount: the regions come out 13, 19 and 30 visitors lower than Georgia's total in 2005, 2013 and 2023 (−0.013, −0.019 and −0.030 thousand). Both are served as Geostat published them. Museum staff (2020–2025) are national only.

## Theatres

Audience, shows and events come from `Events.xls`; theatres by type and seats ("Including rented") from the main indicators workbook. `Events.xls` footnotes the audience row: "*2006-2020 data includes only performances." From 2021 the audience counts the attendance at all events within the country. This is recorded as `theatre_audience_2021` in `data/imports/science-culture-series-breaks.csv` (the crime package's break format): the chart leaves a gap at 2021 with a dashed rule labelled "new counting", the table marks the year, and no change is computed across it. Geostat stores the audience of 2006–2011 at one decimal (thousand) in `Events.xls` (for example 359.9 in 2010 and 404.2 in 2011); those values are served as stored, and the cross-check against the by-region theatre workbook's Georgia row rounds that row to one decimal for 2006–2009, the years it prints with more decimals. The events rows print `-` for 2006–2020; those years produce no row.

## Known differences and series breaks

Where Geostat's printed parts do not add up to its printed total, every row is served as printed and nothing is adjusted. The only mismatch is museum visitors: the 11 regions minus Georgia's printed total is −0.013 thousand in 2005, −0.019 in 2013 and −0.030 in 2023, that is 13, 19 and 30 visitors. They are recorded as `KNOWN_DIFFERENCES` in `prepare.py` and in `lib/data/scienceCulture/validation.ts`, and each must recur with exactly its recorded amount: a changed amount, or a difference in any other year, fails the check. The tolerance of up to 0.2 thousand for regional group tours (rounding) is not a known difference.

One series break is recorded in `data/imports/science-culture-series-breaks.csv` in the crime package's break format: `theatre_audience_2021` (the audience counts performances only to 2020 and all events from 2021, see Theatres). The pages draw a gap and a "new counting" mark there and compute no change across it. Two other published changes are told on the methodology page and are not breaks, because Geostat's tables record neither as a change of definition: the change of collector between the 2019 and 2020 research figures, and the rise of researchers under 25 from 170 in 2020 to 1,012 in 2021. The pages draw nothing at either.

## Gaps, dashes and zeros

A missing value is a gap in the chart, a dash in the table and a blank Excel cell, never zero. In the researcher, R&D staff, patent and staff workbooks `-` means none and is stored as `0` (the rows add up only so; for example `invention_patents.foreign` in 2018). In the four events rows of `Events.xls`, `-` before 2021 means the figure was not collected and produces no row. `…` and blank cells produce no row. Nothing is estimated, interpolated or derived.

## Indicators

IDs are stable lowercase ASCII `science_culture.{measure}.{group}`; labels are display data. Measures: `researchers`, `rd_staff`, `institutions`, `rd_spending`, `invention_applications`, `invention_patents`, `utility_applications`, `utility_patents`, `museums`, `museum_visitors`, `museum_tours`, `museum_exhibitions`, `museum_staff`, `theatres`, `theatre_audience`, `theatre_shows`, `theatre_events`, `theatre_seats` and `theatre_staff`. Regional groups use the site's 11 region IDs (`region_tbilisi` … `region_shida_kartli`). Every row has `basis=actual` and `value_status=official`. Fiscal.ge computes nothing: no totals, shares, ratios, growth rates or interpolation.

## Validation and evidence

`python3 prepare.py --check`, `python3 -m unittest test_validation -v` and `node verify_independent.mjs` in the package rebuild every output, re-read every served cell (the second program with separately written label tables) and reject a changed value, a missing, extra or duplicate observation, a year outside a table's coverage and an unknown row or region label. They require women + men = total (researchers, R&D staff, museum and theatre staff); degrees, fields and age groups = all researchers; researchers + technicians + support + not specified = all R&D staff, and the R&D staff workbook's researchers = the researchers workbook; permanent + contract and the three age groups = all staff; museum types and the regions = all museums; theatre types = all theatres; the regions = Georgia as above; the national visitors (million) = the by-region Georgia row within 0.1 thousand; and the `Events.xls` audience = the by-region theatre workbook's Georgia row. Anchors: researchers 2024 14,046 (women 7,732); R&D staff 2024 16,191; institutions 2024 66; R&D spending 2024 242.9 million GEL; invention applications from Georgian applicants 2025 79, registered 41, international applications 85; museums 2025 261; museum visitors 2025 1,998.924 thousand; theatres 2025 53; theatre audience 2025 620.322 thousand.

## Serving on the Science and culture pages

`npm run data:prepare-science-culture` writes `data/imports/science-culture-annual.csv` (UTF-8 with BOM), `data/imports/science-culture-series-breaks.csv` and `data/reports/science-culture-validation.json`; `npm run data:check-science-culture` rebuilds them in memory and fails on any difference, and runs in `data:validate`. The Prisma mirror `ScienceCultureFact` (migration `20261020090000_science_culture`, row-level security on, public grants revoked) is filled only by the parity-checked `npm run data:import`, and `assertScienceCultureParity` compares CSV and mirror field by field before a database-mode build serves. Pages are prerendered; no request-time read. The science and culture sources are not in the fact-query snapshot, and there are no MCP tools or central publications.

To regenerate, run `python3 prepare.py` in the research package (it rebuilds its `science-culture-annual.csv`, series-breaks file, `coverage.csv` and `validation-report.json` from `official/`, and needs Python 3 with `xlrd` 2.0.x), then `npm run data:prepare-science-culture` in `apps/web`; run `python3 prepare.py --check`, `python3 -m unittest test_validation -v`, `node verify_independent.mjs` and `npm run data:check-science-culture` to verify.

Science and culture's section of the Society and environment group page (`/explorer/society#t-science-culture`; the old `/explorer/science-culture` forwards there) has four cards, each a sparkline of a Georgia series (researchers, invention applications from Georgian applicants, museum visitors, theatre audience) with its latest figure and years. Research, Patents and Theatres are single chart pages for Georgia. Museums is a map index (the 11 regions shaded by the latest year's visitors) with a page for Georgia and each of the 11 regions (`/museums/[id]`, 12 per language). Each page has one latest-value line under its heading, one source line under the chart, no tab row and no measure switch; the series are listed under small headings with every row visible. Where a list mixes units the chart shows one unit at a time: ticking a row of another unit replaces the selection, while rows of the same unit (all the people headings on Research, all patent headings, shows, events and theatres) chart together. The defaults are All researchers, Invention applications from Georgian applicants, Visitors (on every museum page) and Audience, each first in its list, drawn in ink and removable. Counts read whole; visitors, group tours, audience and seats in thousands to one decimal; research spending in million GEL to one decimal. Georgia's museum page adds the six museum types and national staff; a link to another area keeps the selection where the row exists there. A row outside the list is named `Heading — Row`. Every page carries Dataset JSON-LD.

Excel downloads hold Summary, Data and Sources sheets in the reader's language, with the selected unit's series and years, explicit units, blank missing cells and validated links to the archived Geostat workbooks on fiscal.ge (`loadSourcedWorkbookSources("science-culture", locale)`, limited to the eleven served workbooks), listing only the sources of the years and series in the file. Internal cell references are not exported.

Original sources: [Science](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/194) and [Culture](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/615) (Geostat).
