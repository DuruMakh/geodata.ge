# Annual business statistics data collection and validation

## Purpose and status

This methodology governs the research package at `docs/Raw Data/Business/geostat-business-annual/`, collected on 2026-10-15 from the National Statistics Office of Georgia (Geostat). The research captures are immutable. The 4,643 served values, all official, appear on the Business pages under the approved design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-business-design.md`; see [Serving on the Business pages](#serving-on-the-business-pages). Nothing is published through MCP or included in central machine-readable publications.

Geostat is the sole publisher. The package preserves 26 originals under `official/`: the four served survey workbooks (turnover, persons employed, investment in fixed assets, profit and loss), the business demography "Main results" workbook, the survey's eleven other tables and three other business demography workbooks (archived only), `Remark_eng.pdf`, Geostat's two metadata documents (`0502_011026_EN.PDF`, the annual Statistical Survey of Enterprises, certified 1 October 2026; `0519_200225_EN.PDF`, Business Demography, certified 20 February 2025), their Georgian editions (`0502_011026_GE.PDF`, `0519_200225_GE.PDF`) and the two category-page captures. Exact URLs, retrieval times, byte sizes and SHA-256 fingerprints live in `source-manifest.csv` and `source-manifest.json`; the package README holds the full cell map. The metadata documents were added on 2026-10-15 at 11:16 UTC (English) and 11:43 UTC (Georgian) for the definitions the methodology page quotes, in each page language from that language's edition; they supply no value.

## Indicators and definitions

| Indicator IDs | Meaning | Source | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `business.turnover.*` | Turnover: "Income received from sales of goods or services (excluding VAT and excise tax)." Million GEL | 0502EN0101 | 1999–2025; regions 2006–2025 |
| `business.employed.*` | Number of persons employed: "Average number of employees (employed, employed founders and family members employed in the family enterprise) in an enterprise during the accounting period." Persons | 0502EN0103 | 1999–2025; regions 2006–2025 |
| `business.investment.*` | Investments in fixed assets: "Acquisition and capital improvement of tangible fixed assets, excluding the sales and transfers of fixed assets." Million GEL | 0502EN0111 | 2006–2025 |
| `business.profit.*`, `business.loss.*` | Geostat's separate PROFIT and LOSS tables, each a positive amount; the metadata gives no further definition. Million GEL | 0502EN0114 | 2006–2025 |
| `business.active.*`, `business.births.*`, `business.deaths.*` | Active enterprises, enterprise births and enterprise deaths (business register). Enterprises | Main-results | 2014–2024 |
| `business.birth_rate.*`, `business.death_rate.*` | Births or deaths as a percentage of active enterprises, as Geostat prints them | Main-results | 2014–2024; section death rates not 2016 or 2019 |

Survey groups are `total`, `size_large`, `size_medium`, `size_small`, Geostat's 14 activity groups (`sector_a`, `sector_b_e` for Geostat's combined "Industry", `sector_f` … `sector_s`, without K and O) and the 11 regions (`region_tbilisi`, `region_adjara`, …). Firms groups are `total`, the 18 NACE sections A–N and P–S, `sector_unknown` (Geostat's "ACTIVITY UNKNOWN" row), the 11 regions, `region_abkhazeti` and `region_unknown`. Rates exist for `total`, the sections and `sector_unknown`.

The survey (metadata 0502, sections 3.3 and 3.6, and `Remark_eng.pdf` items 1–3) covers non-financial corporations: it excludes NACE sections K (from 2006), O, T and U and group 47.8, and from 2006 non-commercial legal persons and entities of public law. Respondents are limited liability, joint liability and joint stock companies, limited partnerships, cooperatives and individual enterprises, classified by principal activity and actual address. Enterprises are sampled by stratified random sampling and weighted; the stated sampling error is at most 0.5% for the country, 1–2% for regions and 2–3% for economic activities (section 13.2). Firm sizes follow the note under each size sheet: large, more than 249 persons employed and/or an average annual turnover of 60 million GEL; medium, 50 to 250 persons and a turnover of 12 to 60 million GEL; small, no more than 50 persons and a turnover of 12 million GEL. The metadata words the thresholds slightly differently (large: more than 249 persons or a turnover of at least 60 million GEL; small: no more than 49 persons); the pages follow the size-sheet note. The note under the turnover activity sheet (C38) says the turnover of Arts, entertainment and recreation counts the sum of each bet placed by betting firms.

Business demography (metadata 0519) comes from the business register, not the survey. A unit is active if it shows turnover, employment or paid salaries, profit or loss, or taxes other than only property tax during the calendar year; a death is confirmed only after two years of inactivity, and a reactivation after more than two years is a birth (section 18.3). The population excludes sections O, T and U only, so financial firms are included. Births and deaths follow the Main-results footnotes (no other enterprise involved; mergers, break-ups, split-offs, take-overs and restructuring excluded). The data are published on 15 November of the following year (section 14.1), so 2024 is the latest year.

All rows use `basis=actual` and `value_status=official`. Nothing is calculated, interpolated or imputed.

## Identifiers

Indicator IDs are stable lowercase ASCII names `business.{measure}.{group}`; the 284 served IDs are listed in `coverage.csv`. Source IDs are `geostat_business_*` (the registry adds the `source.` prefix), and the series-break ID is `survey_scope_2006`. Georgian and English labels are display data, never identifiers. `data/imports/business-annual.csv` has one row per indicator and year, with the columns `year`, `indicator_id`, `value`, `published_value`, `unit`, `basis`, `value_status`, `source_id`, `source_sheet`, `source_cell` and `last_reviewed_at`. `value` keeps the stored cell token and `published_value` rounds half-up to the cell's number format, using the value as Excel displays it (15 significant digits): one decimal for amounts and rates, none for persons and enterprises.

## Series boundaries

| Boundary | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Survey coverage in 2006 (`survey_scope_2006`, from 2006-01-01; every `business.turnover.*` and `business.employed.*` ID) | `Remark_eng.pdf` item 1: section K is excluded from 2006, and non-commercial legal persons and entities of public law from 2006. Until 2005 the totals include them, and K has no column, so the 1999–2005 sector columns fall short of the total by 1.4–19.1 million GEL (turnover) and 240–449 persons (persons employed). Investment, profit and loss start in 2006. Each series is one line and no change is computed across the break. |
| Regions from 2006 | `Remark_eng.pdf` item 5: until 2006 firms registered in one region but working in several appear only in the country total; from 2006 their data are given by the location of the main office. Survey region values are served from 2006 only. A multi-region firm counts in full in its main office's region, so a region's figure can include work done elsewhere and Tbilisi's can be overstated. |
| Regions short of Georgia | The 11 regions add up to less than Georgia in every year from 2006 for turnover (29.8–170.1 million GEL; 170.1 in 2025), persons employed (901–1,413) and investment (1.1–86.7 million GEL), and in most years for profit and loss. The workbooks print no other region and no note. Each difference is recorded exactly in `KNOWN_DIFFERENCES` (`prepare.py`) and never filled. |
| Firms with unknown activity | Each activity sheet's `ACTIVITY UNKNOWN` row (no section letter) is served as `sector_unknown`: 22,681 of 249,236 active firms and 21,615 of 56,259 births in 2024. Only with it do the 18 sections add up to the total. Its published rates exceed 100% in some years (deaths 628.2% in 2020); they stay in the CSV and mirror but are not listed on the pages. |
| Death rates 2016 and 2019 | The 2016 and 2019 activity sheets have no death-rate column, so the section and `sector_unknown` death rates are gaps in those years; the total's rate comes from the regions sheet every year. |
| Abkhazeti AR and Unknown regions | Part of the firms total and kept in the data; not shown on the pages. With them the 13 region rows add up exactly to the total. |
| Rounding | Sizes and sectors from 2006 differ from the total by up to 0.2 million GEL in 12 measure-years; each is recorded exactly. |

## Extraction and validation

`prepare.py` locates every column by its printed heading and every row by the year in column A, the year heading of a block or the printed section or region name, reads annual rows only (the 2026 quarter rows below the first blank row are never read), and keeps each observation's source ID, sheet and cell. `python3 -B prepare.py --check` rebuilds every output and rejects a changed value, a missing, extra or duplicate observation, a year outside an indicator's coverage, a survey region before 2006, a published value that is not the stored value rounded to the cell's format, parts that do not add up to the total within 0.15 million GEL, 2 persons or exactly for firms (except the recorded differences above), a published rate that is not births or deaths ÷ active × 100 at one decimal, size and region sheets whose totals differ from the activity sheet, and an anchor differing from Geostat: turnover 2025 253,725.5 million GEL (large firms 173,004.5, Tbilisi 200,837.9), persons employed 899,026, investment 8,021.8, profit 27,936.1, loss 7,152.9, and for 2024 249,236 active firms, 56,259 births, 29,634 deaths and a birth rate of 22.6%. `node verify_independent.mjs` re-reads the five served workbooks with SheetJS and checks all 4,643 rows, with negative controls.

## Serving on the Business pages

`npm run data:prepare-business` writes `data/imports/business-annual.csv` (UTF-8 with BOM) and `data/reports/business-validation.json`; `npm run data:check-business` (part of `data:validate`) rebuilds both in memory and fails on any difference. The Prisma mirror table `BusinessFact` (migration `20261015090000_business`) is filled only by the transactional, parity-checked `npm run data:import`; CSV and database modes serve identical rows.

Routes are `/explorer/business` with `/sales`, `/jobs`, `/investment-profit`, `/firms`, `/regions` and `/regions/[area]` (Georgia and the 11 regions, 12 pages), plus `/methodology/business`, each mirrored under `/en`. The sidebar entry sits after Economy. Sales and Jobs list all firms, then the three sizes and 14 industries; Investment and profit nests the sizes and industries under Investment, Profit and Loss; Firms nests the 18 sections (then unknown activity, for counts only) under Active, New and Closed firms. A nested series is named `Parent — Child` in the chart, tooltip and table. The Regions index and area pages show the 11 regions only, not Abkhazeti AR or unknown.

The pages show Geostat's published values and calculate nothing: no net profit, shares, growth rates, totals or filled gaps. Amounts are in current (nominal) lari, not adjusted for inflation, so comparing amounts from different years does not account for price changes. Charts, tables and series lists show amounts in million GEL to one decimal on every page, topic and area pages alike (Georgia included), as Geostat prints them; the latest-value lines and hub cards show billion GEL to one decimal from 1 billion GEL and million GEL below, and the Regions index map and list billion GEL. People are shown in thousands to one decimal, firm counts as whole numbers and rates to one decimal. Missing years are gaps, dashes and blank Excel cells, never zero. The pages describe the figures and state no causes. The firms Rate view lists the total and the 18 sections, not `sector_unknown`; the counts list `sector_unknown` last under each parent. The Georgian terms for investment in fixed assets, «ინვესტიციები ფიქსირებულ აქტივებში», and for active firms, «აქტიური საწარმოები», follow Geostat's Georgian metadata.

Excel downloads hold Summary, Data and Sources sheets in the reader's language, with the selected series and years, amounts in million GEL, persons and firms as whole numbers, empty cells for missing values and validated links to the archived Geostat workbooks the exported values come from (`data/methodology/source-archives/business.csv`). Internal identifiers and cell references are not exported. `/methodology/business` publishes all 26 originals; the HTML captures download as `.txt` files.

## Statistical limitations and future use

Later integration must preserve annual frequency, the 2006 coverage break, the recorded region and pre-2006 differences and the gaps in section death rates. Quarterly values, 2026, the other survey tables, ownership or legal-form breakdowns, industries within a region, enterprise survival and any calculated ratio would each be a separately reviewed addition.

Original sources: Geostat's [Statistical survey of enterprises](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/326/statistical-survey-of-enterprises) and [Business demography](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/69/business-demography) pages, and the [business statistics metadata](https://www.geostat.ge/en/modules/categories/530/metadata-business-statistics).
