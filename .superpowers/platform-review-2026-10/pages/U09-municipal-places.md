# U09-municipal-places review

Unit: `/explorer/municipalities/[slug]` (64 pages) and `/explorer/municipalities/region/[id]` (11 pages), ka and `/en`. Reviewed against the live build at http://localhost:3100. Smallest and largest built municipality pages: akhmeta (211,888 bytes) and borjomi (213,899 bytes); region pages: tbilisi (216,254) and imereti (235,065). The spread inside the family is under 1%, so representative tbilisi plus imereti, adjara, akhmeta and borjomi were probed at 375px and 1280px.

## Summary

Solid, well-tested unit: 84 browser tests and 219 unit tests for it pass, there is no horizontal overflow at 375px on any page probed, the hash parser is safe against hostile input, and the ka/en structure is identical (same data-testid set on both locales). The main problems are duplicated shared pieces (neighbour links, indicators block), internal reconciliation and source fields shipped in every page's RSC payload, and a generic, non-keyboard chart. No critical or high issues.

Scores (1-10): 1 code quality 6 | 2 performance 7 | 3 UI/UX and design 8 | 4 mobile 8 | 5 accessibility 6 | 6 bilingual 8 | 7 SEO and metadata 8 | 8 security 8 | 9 tests 7.
