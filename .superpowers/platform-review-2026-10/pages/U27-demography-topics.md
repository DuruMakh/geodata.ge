# U27-demography-topics review

Pages: `/explorer/demography/age-sex`, `/explorer/demography/births-deaths`, `/explorer/demography/migration` (ka and `/en`). Checked against the live build at http://localhost:3100 on 2026-10-12 at 375, 1280 and 1440 px, in both languages.

## Summary
All three pages work: no console errors, no horizontal overflow at 375px, hash links (including bad or hostile values) are parsed safely, canonical/hreflang pairs are correct and the Excel files carry no internal columns. No critical or high problems. The worst problems are the missing legend on the life-expectancy chart (births-deaths) and no test at all for the national fertility/life charts.
Scores (1-10): code quality 7, performance 7, UI/UX 6, mobile 7, accessibility 7, bilingual 7, SEO 8, security 9, tests 6.
Measured: HTML raw/gzip ka age-sex 407,974/63,283, births-deaths 371,195/43,390, migration 268,802/35,643 bytes (en: 358,420/57,755; 313,081/37,509; 231,489/30,627). Page-specific JS above the /about baseline is about 211 KB raw (62 KB gz) age-sex, 159/46 births-deaths, 182/55 migration (all 14-15 chunks: 807/246, 755/231, 779/239 KB raw/gz in total). `npx playwright test` for the three unit specs: 58 passed in 32.9s with 2 workers; `git status` stayed clean.
