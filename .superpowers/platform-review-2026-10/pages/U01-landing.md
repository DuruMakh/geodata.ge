# U01-landing review (`/` and `/en`)

Reviewed against the live build at http://localhost:3100 on 2026-10-12. Chromium 1194 with swiftshader (the Playwright-bundled browser build was missing, so a throwaway config in /tmp/review-U01-landing/ pointed at /opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194). No repository file was edited.

## Summary

The page is solid: 130 KB / 18 KB gzip HTML, zero console errors, no horizontal overflow at 320/375/768/1024/1440, mobile never requests Three.js, canonical/hreflang/sitemap/JSON-LD are correct, and all 34 tests in `landing.spec.ts` pass. The real defects are accessibility landmarks (header and footer sit inside `<main>`), a 19px desktop hero CTA that breaks DESIGN.md §19, a Georgian dataset-row overflow at 768–850px, and eager prefetching of every explorer route (about 160 KB gzip of extra JS on mobile).

Scores (1–10): code quality 6, performance 6, UI/UX and design 7, mobile 8, accessibility 5, bilingual 8, SEO 8, security 10, tests 6.

Measured numbers:

| What | Georgian `/` | English `/en` |
|---|---|---|
| HTML raw / gzip | 129,803 / 17,952 B | 108,564 / 16,073 B |
| Inline RSC flight data in HTML | about 69.8 KB (54% of the HTML) | not measured |

- First-load JS referenced by the page (modern browsers): 489,561 B raw, 146,673 B gzip, in 8 chunks. A 9th chunk, 112,594 B raw / 39,627 B gzip, is `noModule` and not fetched by modern browsers.
- Desktop-only Three.js chunk (≥768px, loaded at idle after `load`): 581,981 B raw / 147,448 B gzip.
- Total on mobile (375px) without scrolling: 308,815 B of scripts (gzip, transferred) in 23 requests, plus 67 KB of RSC prefetch; after scrolling, 401 KB of scripts and 174 KB of RSC prefetch.
- Fonts: 4 files, 188,764 B. Hero image: 23.6 KB webp (390px).
- Measured in Chromium on localhost: CLS 0.0002 to 0.007; LCP 168–240 ms.
