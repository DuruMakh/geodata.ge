# U29-methodology-about review

Unit: `/methodology`, `/methodology/[dataset]` (17 live articles), `/about`, Georgian and `/en`. Representative page in the brief, `/methodology/budget`, does not exist (HTTP 404, not in sitemap). I used `/methodology/expenditure` (largest HTML, 79 source files), `/methodology/gdp` (smallest article, 5 source files), the hub and `/about`. All measurements are against http://localhost:3100.

## Summary
Page is solid on SEO basics (canonical, hreflang pair, x-default, breadcrumb and Dataset JSON-LD present, KA/EN structure identical on all 17 articles), keyboard use and downloads (all 9 GDP download links return 200; third-party HTML originals are served as `.txt` with nosniff). There is one real phone defect: the article H1 is wider than the screen on two Georgian articles at every phone width up to 412px.
Scores (1-10): code quality 6, performance 7, UI/UX and design 6, mobile 4, accessibility 7, bilingual 8, SEO 7, security 8, tests 6.
Counts: 1 high, 4 medium, 17 low (22 findings, 0 critical).
